Method & Limitations

  • 10 m Sentinel-2 resolution; ponds under the minimum lake-size cutoff are excluded from the inventory.
  • Baseline (Nov 2019-Mar 2020) vs. recent post-monsoon season, matched to reduce seasonal bias — see CHIRPS rainfall ratio KPI.
  • Water = MNDWI>0 in ≥30% of cloud-free observations; built-up from Dynamic World, thresholded at 0.5.
  • No ground-truth field survey; encroachment vs. drying/siltation is inferred from co-located built-up growth only.
